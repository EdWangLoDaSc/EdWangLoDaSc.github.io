Hanyang Wang

hanyangw [at] uchicago [dot] edu

I am an M.S. candidate in Data Science (Artificial Intelligence) at the University of Chicago. My research interests include data efficiency, LLM post-training, agentic reinforcement learning, agent harnesses, and LLM data synthesis.

I am currently working with the LongCat team at Meituan on foundation-model agentic coding. I study how language models can learn more effectively from trajectories, natural-language feedback, and interaction with complex environments.

Previously, I worked on foundation-model post-training at Microsoft AI, reinforcement learning for recommendation at Tencent, LLM application algorithms at ByteDance, and model evaluation at Xiaohongshu.
